## 🌟 Executive Overview

Today’s engineering sprint focused on **two primary objectives**:
1. **End-to-End Operational Stability & Workflow Fixes:** Streamlining the Station House Officer (SHO) command dashboard, field officer assignments, citizen record accuracy, visit management, and role-based permissions.
2. **Flagship Public Portal UI/UX Overhaul:** Upgrading the public-facing landing page into a state-of-the-art, interactive digital gateway with authentic **Delhi Police Royal Blue & Wine Red** aesthetics, live product mockups, and interactive citizen tools.
